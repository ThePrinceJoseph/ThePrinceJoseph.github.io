Home Assistant (personal)

"Home Assistant" is a private, personal Google Cloud app. It connects one household's Home Assistant server to that household's own Google Nest thermostat through Google's Smart Device Management API. It isn't offered to anyone else.

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026.

  • Who uses it: only the owner of this site, for their own home.
  • What it accesses: the owner's Nest device data (temperature, mode, settings) and the ability to change those settings, as granted on Google's sign-in screen.
  • Where the data goes: only to the owner's Home Assistant server at home. Nothing is sold, shared, or sent to any third party.
  • Revoking access: remove "Home Assistant" at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Terms

For the owner's personal use only. No service is offered to the public.
